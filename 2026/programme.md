We are very happy to publish the definitive conference programme of ArcheoFOSS 2026, to be held **in Bologna** on 30 November and 1 December 2026.

Please, feel free to contact us anytime by sending an email to [darchlab@unibo.it](mailto:darchlab@unibo.it).

---

ArcheoFOSS 2026 is hosted by [**DArchLab - Digital Archaeology Laboratory, Dipartimento di Storia Culture Civiltà, Alma Mater Studiorum Università di Bologna**](https://disci.unibo.it/it/con-societa-e-impresa/laboratori-e-centri/lab-5), with the contribution of [**Dipartimento di Storia Culture Civiltà, Alma Mater Studiorum Università di Bologna**](https://disci.unibo.it).
